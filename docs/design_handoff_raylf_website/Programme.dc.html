G2G Millionaires

A programme equipping young founders to build enduring wealth and enterprise.

About the Programme

Building the next generation of African wealth creators.

G2G Millionaires brings young African founders together with mentors, investors and the royal institutions of the continent, so that the success stories celebrated at the RAYLF Awards become enterprises that last for generations.

Participants are drawn from the RAYLF community of 20 to 39-year olds across the globe.
